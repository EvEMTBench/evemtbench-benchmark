Validation-selection reliability

Every reported baseline is chosen on the validation split. This section grades that choice after the fact on the held-out sets, and the grading never feeds back into the choice. Same winner: the validation pick is also best on the reported set. Regret: how far the pick falls short of the best baseline on that set, in metric units (0 = no loss). τ: Kendall τ-b between two rankings of the learned baselines. With five baselines τ is coarse, so read regret first. Adapted from akmaier/Agent4CT's board rank-correlation check.
