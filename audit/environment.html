Determinism & seeds

Every cell aggregates seeds 0–4 (5 seeds); results report mean ± 95% CI half-width (Student t, ddof=1). What is and is not bit-reproducible is documented in docs/determinism.md.

Window memmaps are staged to node-local $TMPDIR before training; sidecar manifests (feature hash, shape, contract fingerprint) travel with the data and are re-verified at load time.
