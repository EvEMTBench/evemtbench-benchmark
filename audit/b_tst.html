What it does. A Time Series Transformer encoder, the attention-based reference: the window is cut into overlapping 16-sample patches that span all channels (59 tokens), and three self-attention blocks relate every patch to every other. It differs from the published model in the strided patch embedding (one token per time step there) and in using 8 attention heads (16 there); the configuration is fixed, not tuned.

Reference: Zerveas et al., KDD 2021 (encoder); Nie et al., ICLR 2023 (patch embedding) · Implementation: src/evemtbench/baselines/tst.py
